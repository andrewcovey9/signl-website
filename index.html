Questions

Straight answers.

Do I have to share my financials?

Not for the AI work. That one is about how work gets done, not what it costs, and there is nothing to prepare. The financial side is the opposite - it runs on your monthly P&L and balance sheet, which is the whole point of it.

Do I have to buy both?

No. Most people start with one. The AI Diagnostic is one price and one decision, so cold enquiries usually start there. If you already have a bookkeeper and just want somebody reading the statements each month, start on the financial side.

What exactly does the guarantee mean?

Find 5-10 hrs/week - or it's free. If the AI Diagnostic we deliver doesn't identify 5 to 10 hours a week of work AI can take off your plate, you don't pay for it. It's judged on what we hand you, not on how much of it you go and implement.

How long does it take?

The discovery session is 30-45 minutes. The AI Diagnostic follows it, then a second session to go through what we found.

Do I need new software for the monthly read?

No. It runs on the monthly P&L and balance sheet you already produce. What you get back is a single file you can keep, forward to your bookkeeper, and open in three years.

Who is this for?

Small businesses where a handful of people carry most of the work, the repetitive parts of it are getting expensive, and nobody has time to sit with the numbers each month. It is not built for enterprises.

Am I signing up for anything ongoing?

Not with the $999. That covers both sessions and the Diagnostic. Anything after it is a separate decision.
