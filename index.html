How it works

Questions people actually ask

Straight answers.

When can I hire my next employee?

When the work is there long enough to cover the seat and the cash is there to carry it through a slow month. That's a number, not a feeling: what the role costs fully loaded, what it has to bill to pay for itself, and how many months of runway you have if the work slows. The monthly read gets you those three, and a runway model tests the hire before you make it.

When should I buy my next truck?

Same test, different asset. Payment plus fuel, insurance and maintenance against the revenue the truck unlocks, checked against your cash position for the months before it pays for itself. Most owners already know the number they want it to hit. The model shows what happens if it takes twice as long to get there.

How can AI actually change my business?

Not by replacing anybody. By taking the jobs that repeat: the first reply to a lead, the quote you rebuild from scratch every time, the invoice follow-up that stops during a busy week. In a small business that is usually five to ten hours a week, and it is the difference between working on the business and working in it.

What are the top five things I can do with AI right now?

Reply to inbound leads within a minute. Build quotes from your own pricing logic instead of retyping them. Chase unpaid invoices on a schedule that does not depend on somebody remembering. Turn a phone call into a written job record. Draft the recurring report from the same sources every week. In that order, because that is usually the payback order.

Does my whole team need AI?

No. Most of the value lands with the two or three people whose week is full of repetition. Rolling a tool out to everybody at once is the usual way this fails.

Do I need an AI specialist on staff?

No. If you need a specialist to keep it running, it was built wrong. What we set up runs on tools your team can operate, and the Diagnostic is written so somebody else could implement it without us.

What does the guarantee actually mean?

Find 5-10 hrs/week - or it's free. If the Diagnostic we hand you does not identify five to ten hours a week AI can take off your plate, you don't pay. It is judged on what we deliver, because that is the part we control.

Do I have to buy both services?

No, and most people don't. They are priced separately and they run separately.
