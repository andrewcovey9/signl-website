Straight answers

What people ask before buying.

What exactly do I get for the $999?

A working quoting workflow, built with you live in a 90-minute session and handed over running - plus the Diagnostic: a written, step-by-step implementation guide covering every other repeating task we found, the tool or workflow that takes it, the order to do them in, and the hours each one gives back. 30 days of tuning are included. Yours to keep and run with or without us.

How is the guarantee judged?

On the Diagnostic we deliver. If it doesn't identify five to ten hours a week AI can take off your plate, you don't pay. What happens after that depends on whether you implement it, which is outside our control - so we don't judge it on that.

Will this replace anyone on my team?

That's not what we're aiming at. Every business we've done this in kept its people and gave them back the part of the week that was repetition.

Do I have to buy tools?

Usually a couple of subscriptions, and we tell you the cost before you commit to any of them. Several of the highest-payback fixes use tools you already pay for.

How long does it take?

Two sessions inside about two weeks - 45 minutes to walk the week, 90 minutes to build the quoting workflow - and the Diagnostic within a week of the second one.

Worst case, you learn where the week goes.

Forty-five minutes to see if there's five to ten hours in your business worth going after.
