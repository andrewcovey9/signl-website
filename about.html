About

We built this for our own businesses first.

Everything we sell is something we needed, built, and still use.

The story

It started as a spreadsheet problem.

We spent years building forecasts and analysis inside companies - originations forecasting, revenue operations analytics, budgets, cash. Big organizations, where a bad forecast is a board conversation. The tools there are good, and expensive, and none of them are pointed at a business with nine trucks.

At the same time we were running small businesses of our own. Same questions, no analyst. When can we hire? Can we carry that payment through February? Which jobs actually made money last quarter? We answered them the long way, by hand, month after month.

So we built the small-business version: the monthly read, the cash forecast, the budget check. First for ourselves, then for the people who asked how we were doing it.

Then the other half

The week was the bigger leak.

Once the numbers were clear, the constraint moved. It wasn't information anymore, it was hours - quotes rebuilt from scratch, leads answered late, invoices chased when somebody remembered, the same figures re-keyed into a second system.

We've now put roughly a thousand hours between us into learning, building and implementing this stuff: AI tools, CRMs, automations, agents, internal apps. Not in a lab. In our own businesses, and in the businesses of people who let us try.

We're still running those businesses today, still using these tools every week. When something we recommend stops working, we find out the same way you would.
