About

Two people who do this work for a living, doing it for smaller businesses.

Signl Advisory is a small Utah practice. We read financial statements for a living and we have spent the last few years putting AI tools into real workflows. Most of our clients are owner-run businesses where a few people carry everything.

Who you work with

You get the two of us, not an account manager.

There is no team behind us and no junior doing the work. When you book a call, you talk to the person who will write your Diagnostic or read your statements.

How we work

What you can expect from us.

  • ✓
    We tell you when the answer is no.Plenty of repetitive work is not worth automating, and plenty of businesses do not need a monthly financial read. We would rather say so on the first call than sell you something that sits unused.
  • ✓
    Everything we hand you is yours.The Diagnostic is a document you keep, take to somebody else, or run yourself. The monthly report is a single file, not a dashboard you have to keep paying for to read.
  • ✓
    We say what the numbers cannot support.Every financial report ends with the checks we ran and an explicit list of what it does not claim. If the statements will not carry a metric, we say so instead of quietly dropping it.
  • ✓
    We are small on purpose.We take on the number of clients two people can do properly. That is the constraint, and it is also the product.

Why both

Why one practice does both of these.

They answer the same question from two directions. The statements tell you where the money and the hours are going; AI is usually the cheapest way to get the hours back; and the statements are how you find out whether it worked. Doing one without the other means either guessing at where to start, or never checking whether the change paid for itself.

Talk to us before you decide anything.

A first call is a conversation about your week and your numbers. No deck.
