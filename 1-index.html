Where the hours are

The same six jobs eat most of the week.

Work that is repetitive enough to hand off and important enough that nobody has. These are what the discovery session usually turns up.

Responding to leads

Inquiries that sit for hours before somebody gets back to them, when the first reply is what wins the job.

Building quotes

The same pricing logic retyped into a new document every time, from scratch, by the person who can least afford the hour.

Chasing invoices

Collections follow-up that runs on memory and sticky notes, and stops entirely during a busy week.

Scheduling and coordination

Back-and-forth to land a time, confirm a job, or reschedule when something moves.

Re-keying data

Information copied by hand between a form, a spreadsheet, and whatever system holds the record of truth.

Putting reports together

The recurring update somebody assembles manually every week or month, from the same sources, in the same order.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do I have to share my financials?

Not for the AI Diagnostic. That one is about how work gets done, not what it costs, and there is nothing to prepare. The Signal Pulse is the opposite - it runs on your monthly P&L and balance sheet, which is the whole point of it.

What exactly does the guarantee mean?

Find 5-10 hrs/week - or it's free. If the AI Diagnostic we deliver doesn't identify 5 to 10 hours a week of work AI can take off your plate, you don't pay for it. It's judged on what we hand you, not on how much of it you go and implement.

How long does it take?

The discovery session is 30-45 minutes. The AI Diagnostic follows it, then a second session to go through what we found.

Can I do the Signal Pulse without the AI Diagnostic?

Yes, and the other way round too. They are separate. Most people who come in cold start with the Diagnostic because it is one price and one decision.

Do I need new software for the Pulse?

No. It runs on the monthly P&L and balance sheet you already produce. The report is a single file you can keep, forward to your bookkeeper, and open in three years.

Who is this for?

Small businesses where a handful of people carry most of the work, the repetitive parts of it are getting expensive, and nobody has time to sit with the numbers each month. It is not built for enterprises.

Am I signing up for anything ongoing?

No. $999 covers both sessions and the AI Diagnostic. Anything after that is a separate decision.
