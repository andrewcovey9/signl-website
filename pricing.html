Straight answers

About the money.

What is one-time and what is monthly?

One-time: the $999 Diagnostic, any AI implementation build ($1,500+), and any custom financial build ($2,500+). Monthly: the financial read at $400, optional care and tuning on AI builds at $250 - $500, and optional maintenance on a financial build at $300. That is the whole list.

Why is the Diagnostic a flat number and the builds a range?

The Diagnostic is the same size job every time: two sessions and a written plan. A build is not - it depends on how many systems have to talk to each other and how clean your data is. You get one number for your build after the first call.

Should I ask for a range or a single number?

Ask for both, from anybody you're considering. A range tells you whether you're in the right neighborhood before you spend an hour on a call. A single number is what you actually sign. Anyone who won't give you the first is wasting your time, and anyone who gives you the second before seeing your books is guessing.

Is there a contract?

Month to month on the monthly work. The Diagnostic is a one-time engagement. Nothing auto-renews into a year.

Can I start small?

Yes, and most do. One Diagnostic, or the monthly read on its own. Everything else is added when it earns its place.

Am I too small for this?

If you're under a few hundred thousand in revenue, the honest answer is usually not yet - and we'll tell you that on the first call rather than sell you something.
