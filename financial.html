Straight answers

What people ask.

Is this bookkeeping?

No. We don't keep your books or file your taxes. We read what your bookkeeper produces and turn it into decisions. If your books are a mess, we'll say so, because nothing downstream works until they're clean.

What do you need from me?

Access to your accounting file, read-only, plus a half hour a month. Everything else is on us.

What does it cost?

$400 a month for the read on your statements, month to month. Custom builds start at $2,500 and run to $5,000, one time each, and keeping a build updated afterwards is an optional $300 a month. Where you land inside the build range depends on how many entities you have, how clean the books are, and how many systems feed them.

Do I need the AI Diagnostic too?

No. They're separate services with separate prices. Plenty of people take one and never the other.
